Research & Student Opportunities

The Shore Innovation Center encourages collaboration between students, faculty, and industry partners through research projects and hands-on learning.

Opportunity Description
Faculty Research Assist professors with technology and engineering research.
Student Projects Participate in interdisciplinary innovation projects.
Industry Partnerships Work with companies on real-world technology solutions.
Innovation Events Attend workshops, networking events, and demonstrations.
